  <div>
    <h2 id="why-pawse">Why I made it.</h2>
    <p>I tried a number of focus timers, but never found one that felt quite at home on the Mac or handled the end of a session the way I wanted. So I made a small one for myself.</p>
    <p>Instead of interrupting the moment Focus ends, Pawse shows a quiet reminder and waits. Click it when you are ready, or pause the keyboard and pointer for a moment. If you immediately start working again, Pawse backs out and waits for the next pause.</p>

  <div>
    <h2 id="break-screen">When you stop, it steps in.</h2>
    <p>A break can use your wallpaper or an image you choose. It fills every display, keeps an Emergency Exit available, and cleans up when the break is over.</p>
    <p>As I kept using Pawse, I wanted breaks to feel a little less rigid without making them easy to ignore. Discreet Mode can leave the desktop visible while a committed break continues. End Break Early lets me move on normally after a minimum amount of rest, instead of counting it as an Emergency Exit.</p>

<section class="story-note" aria-labelledby="privacy">
  <h2 id="privacy">Nothing leaves your Mac.</h2>
  <p>Settings and session history stay local. Pawse has no account, cloud sync, telemetry, advertising, or networking feature. Natural-break detection reads only aggregate system idle time—not keys, pointer positions, app names, windows, or screen contents.</p>
