## Is Pawse free?

Yes. Pawse is open source under the MIT License. Donations do not unlock features.

## Which Macs are supported?

macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon or Intel.

## Does Pawse need an account or internet?

No. Pawse has no account, networking, cloud sync, ads, or telemetry.

## Does it record my input?

No. Natural-break detection uses aggregate activity only; it does not record keys, pointer positions, apps, or screen contents.
