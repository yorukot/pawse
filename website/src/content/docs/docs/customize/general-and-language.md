## General

- **Launch at Login:** add or remove Pawse from login items.
- **Reset Settings to Defaults:** reset preferences without deleting history.

## Language

Available languages: Automatic, English, Español, 日本語, 繁體中文, and 简体中文.

Changing language restarts Pawse:

- Active Focus is recorded as interrupted.
- Active Break is recorded as an Emergency Exit.
- A pending break is canceled without a record.

If automatic restart fails, reopen Pawse manually.
