---
title: Analytics
description: View and clear local session history.
---

Open **Pawse → Analytics**.

Analytics includes:

- Focus and Break time and counts
- Completion rate and Emergency Exits
- Daily chart and recent sessions

Use the toolbar to change the date range. Sessions may be completed, interrupted, skipped, or ended through Emergency Exit.

Use the trash button or **Privacy → Clear Analytics Data** to delete history. Settings and the current cycle are unchanged.

All Analytics data stays on your Mac.
