---
title: End Break Early
description: End a break normally after a minimum rest time.
---

![End Break Early, Discreet Mode, and Emergency Exit on the Break screen](/assets/pawse-break-latest.webp)

1. Start a Short or Long Break.
2. Wait until **End Break Early** becomes available.
3. Select it to continue the normal cycle.

The default minimum is 30 seconds. Set it from 10 seconds to 120 minutes under **Break Behavior**, or turn off **Allow Ending Breaks Early**.

Unlike **Emergency Exit**, End Break Early records the break as skipped and follows the normal post-break settings.
